7. HEART CURVE

---

Implicit heart:
(x^2+y^2-1)^3-x^2*y^3=0

Parametric heart:
x(t)=16sin(t)^3
y(t)=13cos(t)-5cos(2t)-2cos(3t)-cos(4*t)

Parameter range: 0 to 2*pi

8. BUTTERFLY CURVE

---

r(t)=exp(cos(t))-2cos(4t)-sin(t/12)^5
x(t)=sin(t)*r(t)
y(t)=cos(t)*r(t)

Parameter range: 0 to 12*pi

9. FLOWER CURVES

---

10. SPIRALS

---

11. INFINITY SYMBOL

---


x(t)=cos(t)/(1+sin(t)^2)
y(t)=sin(t)*cos(t)/(1+sin(t)^2)

Parameter range: 0 to 2*pi

12. ELLIPSE

---


x(t)=4cos(t)
y(t)=2sin(t)

IMPORTANT NOTES


- Start testing with sin(x), cos(x), or x^2.
- Most trigonometric functions use radians.
- Equations containing only x generally work in a y=f(x) graph.
- Circle, heart, butterfly, flower, spiral, and infinity curves may require implicit or parametric graph support.
- A simple y=f(x) graph cannot directly display all parametric curves.
